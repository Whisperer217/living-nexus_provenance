## Governing Classification

| Classification | Meaning | Public-use posture |
|---|---|---|
| **FACT** | Directly located in code, test, documentation, or authoritative source. | May be stated with source and scope. |
| **TECHNICAL CAPABILITY** | Demonstrable platform behavior, not a legal conclusion. | May be stated with operational limit. |
| **CREATOR DECLARATION** | A creator statement preserved by the platform. | Must remain labeled as declaration. |
| **DOCTRINE** | Living Nexus belief, design law, or intended posture. | May be stated as doctrine, not as proof. |
| **LEGAL POSITION** | A conclusion concerning rights, compliance, enforceability, or liability. | Counsel review required. |
| **ASPIRATION** | Intended future capability or outcome. | Do not present as existing. |
| **UNSUPPORTED** | Not presently backed by adequate evidence. | Do not use publicly. |
| **UNKNOWN** | Evidence is insufficient or conflicting. | Investigate; do not infer. |

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

1. A timestamp does not equal legal authorship.
2. Provenance does not equal ownership.
3. A WID does not equal copyright registration, trademark, patent, or legal protection.
4. AI disclosure does not equal a copyrightability or authorship determination.
5. Creator sovereignty is doctrine until its product, export, contractual, and infrastructure controls are demonstrated.
6. Preservation is not permanence until custody, export, recovery, and continuity evidence exists.
7. A registry record is not an adjudication.
