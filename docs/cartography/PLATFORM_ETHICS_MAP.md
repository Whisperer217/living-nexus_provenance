## Ethical Classification Rule

No ethical row becomes a **technical guarantee** until a reproducible implementation control is identified. No ethical row becomes a **legal claim** until counsel establishes the applicable legal position. This is especially important for consent, permanence, ownership, protection, AI training, and rights language.
