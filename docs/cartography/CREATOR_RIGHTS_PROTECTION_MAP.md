# E. Creator Rights & Protection Map

> **Legal status:** This is a technical and claim-boundary map, not legal advice. Rights, remedies, contract interpretation, jurisdiction, and enforcement require qualified counsel.

| Protection area | What Living Nexus can technically provide now | What it cannot establish alone | External dependency / next evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Creator/work records, WID-linked values, timestamps, declared metadata, and exports where implemented. | That a record proves legal authorship, priority against all parties, or admissibility/weight in any proceeding. | Issuance-path, time-source, key custody, export, and verification test evidence. |
| Provenance | A platform record of declared and system-recorded fields; canonicalization/hash/signature utility; potential WID verification route. | Complete real-world creation history, prior chain of title, or all off-platform transformations. | Provenance event and witness trace; possible interoperable standard assessment. |
| Attribution | Public identity/profile/credits/participation and HAAI display fields. | Accurate or complete authorship without corroboration. | Creator declaration policy; contributor dispute workflow; evidence classification. |
| Copyright | Supports creator documentation and clear public scope limitations. The public Terms correctly state that WIDs do not replace official copyright registration. | Copyright ownership, registrability, infringement, or remedies. | Copyright Office process and counsel. The Office’s AI materials emphasize case-specific human authorship and registration disclosure questions.[1] [2] |
| Trademark / marks | Can display creator handles, platform marks, and declared branding. | Mark ownership, registrability, likelihood of confusion, abandonment, or false-association outcome. | Trademark clearance, filing, policing, license controls, and counsel. |
| Patent | Can preserve technical/creative declarations and timeline context. | Inventorship, novelty, patentability, filing priority, or claim scope. | USPTO process and counsel. The USPTO states that AI may assist, but only natural persons may be named inventors.[3] |
| Contract | Can present terms, contributor/developer terms, and store license choices. | A contract’s validity, enforceability, interpretation, or counterparty compliance. | Accepted-version logs, jurisdictional analysis, counterparty agreement, and counsel. |
| License | Can store declared permission choices and route to purchase/download controls. | That the declarant owns enough rights to grant the license or that a license resolves every downstream right. | Contract, chain-of-title evidence, takedown/dispute process, and counsel. |
| Access control | Can gate drafts, downloads, purchases, and authenticated surfaces. | That data cannot be copied, screen-captured, extracted, or accessed under third-party hosting/provider terms. | Security tests, provider contracts, monitoring, and incident response. |
| Preservation | Can retain records, exports, versions, and backups subject to actual implementation. | Absolute permanence, future readability, or survival of every provider/platform failure. | Tested export/recovery, storage redundancy, migration plan, custodial governance. |
| Enforcement | Can create reports, moderation, status restrictions, notices, and account actions. | Governmental, judicial, platform-wide, or cross-border enforcement. | Contractual remedies, counsel, court/agency processes, and external-platform cooperation. |

## Rights-Safe Language

**May say with evidence:** “Living Nexus records the declared metadata and technical provenance fields described on this record.”

**May say with a clear limitation:** “A WID may support a documented platform timeline; it does not replace official registration or determine legal ownership.”

**Must not say without counsel/evidence:** “This WID legally protects your copyright,” “this record proves ownership,” “this work is fully cleared,” “this content is permanently preserved,” or “this disclosure establishes human authorship.”
