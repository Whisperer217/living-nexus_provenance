# Intentional Interface Doctrine
## Living Nexus Design Law
**Enacted:** 16 August 2026 · **Status:** Canonical · **Domain:** The Doctrine → Design System → Spatial Grammar

---

## The Standard

> Nothing exists merely for decoration. Every object, movement, transition, relationship, and control must communicate the state, history, ownership, or experience of a registered work.

That is the constraint around this rebuild.

Everything on the platform is intentional.

- Not animation for animation's sake.
- Not 3D because 3D is fashionable.
- Not a button because SaaS platforms have buttons.

Every interaction should communicate something about the thing being represented.

---

## What This Prevents

The danger is **beautiful but meaningless**.

We do not want: *Whoa, cool Three.js.*

We want: *Oh. I understand what I'm looking at.*

The beauty should emerge from the meaningful structure.

---

## The Keep / Cut Test

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| An animation looks cool but does not communicate anything | **Cut it.** |
| A unusual interaction makes the provenance concept clearer | **Keep it.** |
| A conventional control is more intuitive than a spatial interaction in that case | **Use the conventional control.** |

Intentional does not mean complicated.

It means nothing accidental.

---

## The Life of a Work

Profile → Edit → Register → Witness

Those are not arbitrary UI sections. They are actions in the life of a work. The spatial interface exists to make those actions tangible.

Then:

Explore → grab the work → drop it into Player → experience the registered artifact.

And the reverse:

Drop a local file into Register → inspect → establish the record.

Same spatial verb. Opposite direction. One grammar.

---

## Spatial Grammar

| Interaction | What it communicates |
|---|---|
| Drag a work | You are moving an artifact. |
| Drop it into Register | You are declaring it. |
| A Witness appears | Someone attested to that event. |
| Follow the lineage | You are traversing history. |
| Load the work into Player | You are experiencing the registered artifact. |
| The work revolves | It is active / in playback. |
| Camera moves toward a node | You are entering that record or domain. |
| Camera pulls back | You are seeing the larger lineage. |
| A connection illuminates | A relationship is being revealed. |
| A new version branches | The work changed without erasing its history. |

Sound, motion, lighting, and transitions may carry semantic meaning. If they do not, they do not ship.

---

## State determines the verb

The same grab is not the same act. The object's state decides what dropping it means.

| State | What the object is | The verb it allows |
|---|---|---|
| Sketch | Incomplete | Carry to Register to declare it. |
| Working | In formation | Carry to Register to declare it. |
| Registered | A sealed record | Carry to Player to experience it. |
| Witnessed | Attested | The teal mark is that fact. |

Player refuses an unregistered work. Register refuses a work already declared. That refusal is the teaching.

Connections do not travel until a relationship is being revealed — hover, load, ceremony, or lineage. Perpetual motion without cause is decoration.

---

## Subordination

The Three.js layer visualizes the registry. It is not the registry.

A spatial interaction that cannot be explained as a provenance act is decoration. Decoration is prohibited.

When a conventional control communicates the act more clearly than a spatial one, the conventional control is the doctrine-aligned choice.
