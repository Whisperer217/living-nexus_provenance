---
name: Artifact Display Priority: Front and Center
use_when: When designing the display of 'artifacts' (e.g., creative works, products) on the platform, especially in relation to other content like 'testimony' or creator profiles.
---

When displaying artifacts on the platform, they should be positioned **front and center** and not buried under testimony or anything else. All artifacts need to be inline with everything else, equally prominent. This preference should guide how products are showcased alongside creator profiles.
