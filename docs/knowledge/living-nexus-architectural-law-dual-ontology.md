---
name: Living Nexus Architectural Law: Dual Ontology for Artifacts
use_when: When designing the architecture, data model, or intellectual property management for the Living Nexus platform, especially concerning the identification and categorization of artifacts.
---

Every artifact in Living Nexus shall declare both its **domain identity** (what it is) and its **implementation identity** (how it fulfills its purpose). Neither identity may be inferred solely from its filesystem location. The implementation ontology is always subordinate to the domain ontology.
