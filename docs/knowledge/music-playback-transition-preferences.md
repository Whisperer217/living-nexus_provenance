---
name: Music Playback Transition Preferences
use_when: When designing or implementing music playback features on the platform, especially regarding transitions between tracks.
---

The user prefers music playback with **fade in and fade out effects**, seamless transitions between tracks, and no periods of silence in the global player. The platform should offer options to toggle these features. The user is also interested in building conceptual albums with blending and transitional effects for seamless playback.
