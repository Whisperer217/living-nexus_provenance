---
name: Living Nexus Core Value Proposition: Provenance Infrastructure for Digital Creations
use_when: When discussing the core purpose, value proposition, or foundational infrastructure of the Living Nexus platform.
---

The Living Nexus platform exists to provide foundational infrastructure for managing the **provenance debt** inherent in digital platforms. This includes reliably identifying creators, preserving attribution, documenting provenance, and tracing relationships between works, especially as creative production accelerates through AI and digital tools.
