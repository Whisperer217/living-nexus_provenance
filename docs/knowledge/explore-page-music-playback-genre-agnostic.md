---
name: Explore Page Music Playback Preference: Genre-Agnostic Continuous Playback
use_when: When designing or updating the music playback functionality on the 'Explore' page, especially concerning queued songs and genre filtering.
---

On the Explore page, queued songs should **not be bound to specific genres** - all songs should play continuously and at random, independent of the filtering system. There should be no cap on the number of songs displayed. Filters should remain persistent. The user prefers a switch for non-randomization and the ability to filter by creator. Display options should include 'list view', 'normal view', or 'creator card view'.
