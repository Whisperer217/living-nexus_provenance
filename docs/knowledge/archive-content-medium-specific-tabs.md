---
name: Archive Content Organization: Medium-Specific Tabs
use_when: When organizing and displaying different types of content within the user's archive or content management system, or on discovery pages like the 'Explore' page.
---

When organizing content within the archive or on discovery pages, each medium (e.g., songs, books) should have its own dedicated **tab or column**, rather than grouping all works under a single generalized tab like 'My Works'. The user also prefers to show maximum content without sliders, and dislikes the term 'content types'.
