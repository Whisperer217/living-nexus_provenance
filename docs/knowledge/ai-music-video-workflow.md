---
name: AI Music Video Workflow: Single Track Upload to Cinematic Mode
use_when: When discussing the workflow for AI-generated music videos on the platform, from single track upload to display in cinematic mode.
---

When a single track is uploaded, the AI should process its metadata, lyrics, and caption to produce a script. This script will then be used to generate a music video loop from the original artwork, which will be displayed in cinematic mode for the track.
