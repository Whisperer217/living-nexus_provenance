---
name: Cosmos Playback Behavior: Seamless Audio Playback with Non-Audio Skip
use_when: When designing or implementing the 'Cosmos' feature, especially concerning content playback and the handling of mixed media types.
---

When playing content within the 'Cosmos' feature, audio tracks should play seamlessly without stopping after each track. If a non-audio file (e.g., a comic book) is present in the Cosmos, it should be displayed but **skipped in the playback queue**, ensuring only audio files are played.
