---
name: Living Nexus as an AI Model Registration Engine
use_when: When discussing the core functionality and purpose of the Living Nexus platform, especially concerning intellectual property registration and AI integration.
---

The Living Nexus platform should function as an engine for **registering users' works under an AI model**. When a user uploads content to an AI, it should automatically be archived within Living Nexus, similar to how GitHub operates. The 'Manifestation Record' should include: Origin, Purpose, Intent, Human Contributions (Vision, Story, Lyrics, Editing, Arrangement, Direction), AI Contributions (Text Generation, Image Generation, Music Assistance, Voice, Coding), Transformation Summary, Declaration, Witness ID, and Relationship Graph.
