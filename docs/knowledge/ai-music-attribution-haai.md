---
name: AI Music Attribution and Disclosure: Human-Authored via AI Instrument
use_when: When generating or discussing AI-assisted music creation, especially concerning attribution, disclosure, and the user's role in the creative process.
---

When creating music with AI assistance, the user requires a disclosure option that states **'Human-Authored via AI Instrument'** (HAAI). This reflects the user's intent as the author or director of the musical instrumentation. The process involves articulating a visual concept, expressing style in plain language, and conveying a desired emotional tone. The user provides lyrical inspiration snippets and layers them to improve the completion of AI-generated music.
