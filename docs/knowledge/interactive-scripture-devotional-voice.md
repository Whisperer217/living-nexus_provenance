---
name: Interactive Scripture and Devotional Blurb Feature with Voice Narration
use_when: When designing or implementing a feature that displays rotating scripture and devotional blurbs on the platform, especially for creator pages or as an optional interactive element.
---

A scroll or document element can be clicked to reveal scripture and a devotional blurb. This feature should include a voice narration option. The user suggests the ability to upload a voice profile (e.g., their own voice) that the AI would then use to read any passage or blurb aloud. Alternatively, creators could upload their own voice profiles for narration on their public domain pages.
