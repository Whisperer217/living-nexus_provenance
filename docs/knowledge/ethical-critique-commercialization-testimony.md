---
name: Ethical Critique of Commercialization of Lived Testimony
use_when: When discussing the ethical implications of commercializing personal experiences, especially in relation to AI, product development, and intellectual property.
---

The user holds a strong ethical critique against companies and businesses that build products and services by appropriating, reusing, and recycling individuals' **lived testimony** without proper attribution or consideration for the chain of custody. This is viewed as a fundamental issue where personal experiences are commodified, leading to a 'war of attribution'.
