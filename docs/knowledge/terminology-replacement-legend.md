---
name: User's Terminology Replacement and Legend Requirement
use_when: When discussing or presenting the user's concepts, especially when using terms that replace common industry or social terms (e.g., 'follow' replaced by 'witness').
---

The user has a preference for replacing common terms with their own specific terminology (e.g., 'follow' is replaced by **'witness'**). When these terms are used, a legend or glossary should be provided to explain the user's specific definitions to the audience.
