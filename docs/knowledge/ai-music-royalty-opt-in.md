---
name: AI Music Generation Royalty System for Opt-in Training
use_when: When discussing the business model for AI music generation, particularly regarding creator compensation for AI model training.
---

Creators who opt-in to allow their creations to train AI models should receive **royalties** based on their contributions to the AI system's training when the platform generates a certain amount of money. This system is designed to provide reciprocal value for creators' 'epistemic work' and differentiate the platform from other AI companies.
