name: Platform Terms of Service Philosophy: Creator Protection and Divine Conviction
use_when: When discussing the platform's terms of service, its ethical framework, intellectual property protection, or the philosophical underpinnings of creator rights.
---

The platform's terms of service are designed to **protect creators, not the platform**. They are founded on the conviction that human creative work carries inherent dignity due to its divine origin, which surpasses legal instruments. The terms invoke existing law where it protects creators and establish a covenant above the law where it fails them. The TOS should also include a platform dependency section.
