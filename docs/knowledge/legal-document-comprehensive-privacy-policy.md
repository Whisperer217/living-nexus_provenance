---
name: Legal Document Preference: Comprehensive Privacy Policy and User Agreement
use_when: When drafting or implementing legal or policy documents for a user's website or application.
---

When creating legal or policy documents, the user requires a **privacy policy** and **user agreement** that are as comprehensive as possible. When presenting these documents or comparing them to others, highlight the clarity and lexicon used, contrasting with other platforms that often have lengthy and complex TOS/TOU/Privacy Policies.
