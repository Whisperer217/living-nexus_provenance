The user prefers a subtle 'Archive Network' background with sacred geometric linework, ancient manuscript etchings, cartographic lines, fine constellation paths, and cathedral architecture, along with a procedural parchment texture. Very subtle thin antique gold chain links should weave throughout the page at 2-4% opacity. Broken chains represent public domain or liberated works; complete chains symbolize verified provenance. Background layers: (1) Pure black, (2) Paper grain, (3) Sacred geometry at 2%, (4) Chain of Witness linework at 3%, (5) Soft radial illumination, (6) Content cards, (7) Typography.
