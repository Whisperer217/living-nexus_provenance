The **Living Nexus Archive (LNA)** is a future concept representing a long-term vision for data archiving and interconnected systems, specifically designed to ensure that creator testimony is recognized as a living entity that artificial systems must license from. **Witness Records** is associated with LNA - an up-to-date list of Witness IDs (WIDs) with minimal identifying information, routing to the original song and creator, including image, song, video, and lyrics.
