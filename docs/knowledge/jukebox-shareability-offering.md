---
name: Jukebox Shareability and 'Leave an Offering' Placement
use_when: When designing or implementing the 'jukebox' feature on the user's platform, especially regarding shareability and the placement of interactive elements.
---

Each playing or queued jukebox item needs a unique shareable link, independent of a code. A **'leave an offering'** button should be prominently placed at the top of the queued jukebox, visually highlighted with a gold border. The jukebox should persist and continue playback after a song is finished in a 'listen together' context.
