---
name: Creator-Controlled Content Order on Homepage and Explore Page
use_when: When designing or updating the display of content on the homepage or explore page, especially when creators have uploaded multiple items.
---

Creators should be able to **rearrange the display order** of their uploaded content on both the homepage and the explore page. This reordering should be reflected consistently across these pages, including the side pane window on the right, based on the creator's preferences. The order should persist after upload.
