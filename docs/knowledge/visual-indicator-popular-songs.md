---
name: Visual Indicator for Popular Songs
use_when: When displaying songs on the platform, especially when a song has achieved a certain level of popularity (e.g., play count).
---

A gold banner or gold border should appear around songs that have surpassed a predefined threshold of plays (e.g., over 50 plays). A **play** is defined as a track playing for at least 30 seconds in the global player. This serves to highlight popular content.
