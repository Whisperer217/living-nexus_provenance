## What This Is

The Living Nexus Language Specification (LNLS) is the canonical reference for every word, concept, object, and design decision on the Living Nexus platform. It is not a style guide. It is not a marketing document. It is the platform's doctrine encoded as language.

Every engineer, designer, and AI agent working on this platform must read this specification before making decisions. The vocabulary defined here is not optional. The principles defined here are constraints, not suggestions.

---

## The Core Doctrine

> *Words have power. Power has meaning. Meaning is what changes the internal state of the witness.*

This is the sentence that governs every language decision on this platform. When we choose "Witness" over "Follow," we are making a philosophical claim about what it means to acknowledge another person's creative record. When we choose "Register" over "Upload," we are making a claim about the nature of the act. When we refuse to ask "Was AI used?" and instead ask "Who participated?", we are making a claim about the nature of authorship.

Language is architecture. Every word is a load-bearing wall.

## Quick Reference: The Most Important Rules

| Rule | Canonical | Prohibited |
|---|---|---|
| Never ask about AI | "Who participated?" | "Was AI used?" |
| Never call creators users | "Creator" | "Content Creator," "User" |
| Never call following following | "Witness" | "Follow," "Subscribe" |
| Never call uploading uploading | "Register Work" | "Upload," "Post" |
| Never call metadata provenance | "Provenance" is the human record | "Metadata" is technical data |
| Never call archiving deleting | "Archive" | "Delete," "Remove," "Hide" |
| Never use binary authorship | "Participation Chain" | "Human-made" vs "AI-made" |

## The Four Arms

Living Nexus has four structural arms, modeled on laminin:

1. **The Registry** — Cryptographic provenance. The WID system. The Creator's claim to their work.
2. **The Community** — Witness relationships. The social layer that gives the Registry credibility.
3. **The Commerce Layer** — Gifts, splits, licensing. The mechanism by which the Registry produces economic value.
4. **The Archive** — Long-term preservation. The living record of human creative contribution.
