## Executive finding

The public-record evidence ties the current Dacr product to **DACR INC.**, an active Florida corporation filed on **2025-01-10**. The record identifies **Solomon Engel** as President and **Ena Celina Engel** as Vice President, while a public launch announcement by Solomon Engel describes the organization’s unveiling of “Digital Assertive Copyright (Dacr).” This supports a leadership linkage, but the reviewed sources do **not** establish a complete legal founder roster. [1] [6]

The operating product is demonstrably newer than the dacr.com domain’s older archive history. A February 2025 archived page showed only a copyright-oriented holding message; Apple’s public metadata dates the Dacr app’s release to **2025-03-29**; Solomon Engel’s launch announcement is dated **2025-05-20** in the search record; and independent coverage described an official launch on **2025-06-01**. These are different milestones and should not be compressed into one claimed “launch date.” [8] [11] [6] [12]

## Founders and operating leadership

The strongest public record is officer-level: the Florida entity lists Solomon Engel and Ena Celina Engel in President and Vice President roles. Solomon Engel also authored the public Dacr unveiling. A public professional-profile search result characterizes him as “Founder and CEO,” but the profile itself required authentication during this review; that label is therefore corroborative context rather than a fully reviewed primary disclosure. [1] [6]

The correct research conclusion is not “Solomon Engel is the sole founder.” It is: **public records strongly connect Solomon Engel to Dacr’s leadership and launch, but a complete founder roster is not verified by the materials reviewed.**

## Patents, trademarks, and claim boundaries

Dacr’s official About page uses the phrase **“PATENT PENDING.”** Its Terms call “Dacr,” “Digital Assertive Copyright,” and related brand elements trademarks of Dacr. These are **company legal/marketing declarations**. [2] [3]

The public USPTO checks returned no results for an exact-wordmark search for **“Dacr”** and no records for an assignee-name patent search for **“Dacr.”** [5] [10] Those results do not prove the absence of a trademark or patent portfolio. A filing could use another applicant/owner spelling, a design mark, a different mark, an earlier entity, an unpublished application, a foreign jurisdiction, or a later assignment.

## Important product-claim tension

The current app listing and marketing materials use expansive language about copyright protection, permanent records, proof of ownership, and blockchain-backed validity. [2] [4] Apple metadata repeats these product descriptions. [11] At the same time, Dacr’s Terms state that the service makes **no representation or warranty** that submitting, storing, or sharing content through the service grants legal rights in any jurisdiction, and says Dacr is not a law firm. [3]

This is not a legal conclusion about the product. It is a documented **claim-boundary tension**: product messaging, technical recordkeeping, creator declaration, independently verifiable evidence, and legal rights must be kept separate when evaluating Dacr or comparing it with another provenance system.
