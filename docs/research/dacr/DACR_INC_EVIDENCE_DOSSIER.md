That combination supports the conclusion that Solomon Engel was publicly leading the product launch and held the recorded President role. It does **not** establish that he was the sole founder, identify every founder, prove equity ownership, or verify the claimed three-year development period. ## Product-claim boundary

The App Store description and official materials present Dacr as producing copyright-protection, public-record, timestamp, certificate, blockchain, and anti-piracy functionality. [2] [4] [8] The company’s Terms are more limited: they say Dacr is not a law firm and make no representation that submitting, storing, or sharing content through the service grants rights provided by law. [3]

This is a material boundary, not a conclusion about legal validity. It means any comparison of Dacr with a provenance or rights platform must keep these layers distinct:

1. **Technical fact:** What record, timestamp, certificate, identifier, or storage event the platform creates.
2. **Creator declaration:** What a user represents about authorship, ownership, timing, or authorization.
3. **Independent evidence:** What a third party can inspect and verify.
4. **Legal claim:** What ownership, registration, enforceability, damages, or jurisdictional consequence a qualified legal authority may determine.
